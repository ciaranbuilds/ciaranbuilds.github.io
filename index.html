Case study

From manual chaos to a system that runs itself — so a charity's team can spend their time on the people who need them.

Cancer support charity · Northern Ireland

A Northern Ireland charity providing practical support to cancer patients and their families — help with finances, respite care, and getting to hospital appointments — was drowning in admin. Every support request that came in through their website landed in someone's inbox and had to be handled by hand. Time that should have been spent supporting vulnerable people was being eaten up by process.

We built a fully automated request management system on Microsoft's Power Platform. From the moment someone submits a request, the system takes over — categorising the need, routing it correctly, storing everything securely in Dataverse, and surfacing it to staff through a purpose-built Power Apps interface. No spreadsheets. No missed requests. No chasing.

Now every submission is triaged and visible to the team immediately. People in difficult circumstances get a faster, more consistent response — and the charity can handle significantly more requests without increasing headcount.

What we built
  • Power Automate flows handling intake, categorisation, routing, email communications, and distribution and tracking of monetary vouchers
  • Microsoft Dataverse for secure, structured, auditable data storage
  • Model-driven Power Apps case management interface for staff
The impact

Faster response times for patients in need. More requests handled consistently, with no manual triage. Staff freed to focus on the people they're there to support — on existing M365 licences, at a fraction of the cost of a custom build.
